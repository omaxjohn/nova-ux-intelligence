# Privacy policy

Effective: 2026-09-09

Nova UX Intelligence is a skills-only plugin. The plugin package contains instructions, references, tests and visual assets. It does not operate a developer-controlled server, require a separate account, place cookies or collect, retain, sell or share personal data with the plugin developer.

Prompts, files and tool activity are processed by the ChatGPT or Codex product in which the plugin is used and remain subject to that product's terms, privacy controls and workspace policies. If a future release adds a connector, hosted service or developer-controlled data processing, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Privacy questions may be submitted through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/omaxjohn/nova-ux-intelligence/issues).
